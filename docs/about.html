Scoring

Each guess scores how many times off it was: the bigger number divided by the smaller. Half or double are both 2×. Your day’s total is the geometric mean of the three, and one guess counts as at most 1,000×.

Sources

Answers come from census data, ministries, the Election Commission, ISRO and official sports records. Each one names its source and year. Spot an error? Tell us.

Your data

Games and stats stay in this browser. To show how others guessed, each guess is also sent with a random ID, not your name or email.
