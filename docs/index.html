How to play

  1. Make one guess per question

    Type it any way you like: 45000, 4.5 lakh, 2 crore, 3 million. A calculator and rough-work pad are built in.

  2. See how far off you were

    Your score is how many times off you were. Guess 50 when the answer is 100 and you are 2× off. Guess 200 and you are also 2× off. 1× is perfect.

  3. Add up the day and share it

    Your day’s total combines all three. Share the card in your group and compare.

Questions people ask

When does the new puzzle come out?

Every night at 12:00 midnight India time.

Can I play puzzles I missed?

Yes. The archive has every puzzle since launch. Only the daily puzzle counts toward your streak.

Is using the calculator cheating?

No. Working it out is the whole point. Looking up the answer is the only thing that spoils it.

Where do the answers come from?

Government reports, census data, official bodies and reliable records. Each answer names its source and year. Some numbers are estimates themselves, and we say so.

Can I suggest a question?

Yes. Send us your question, ideally with a source for the answer.
