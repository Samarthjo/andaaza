What’s an andaaza?

A guess you can reason out. Around 499 CE, Aryabhata worked out that a circle 20,000 across is about 62,832 around. That is π ≈ 3.1416, and he called it āsanna: nearly.

FAQ

  • When does the new puzzle come out?

    Every night at 12:00 midnight India time.

  • Can I play puzzles I missed?

    Yes. Every past puzzle is in the archive. Only the daily one counts toward your streak. Open the archive

  • Is using the calculator cheating?

    No. Working it out is the point. Looking up the answer is the only cheat.

  • Where do the answers come from?

    Census data, ministries, the Election Commission, ISRO and official sports records. Every answer names its source and year.
